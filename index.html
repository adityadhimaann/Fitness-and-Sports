Aerial Yoga:

Aerial yoga combines traditional yoga poses with the use of a fabric hammock suspended from the ceiling. Practitioners use the hammock to support and deepen their yoga poses, allowing for greater flexibility, strength, and decompression of the spine. It adds an element of acrobatics and suspension training to the practice of yoga.
